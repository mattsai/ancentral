Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

AN Central ("we") operates an internal tool, AN Central Publisher, that publishes videos we produce to our own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook).

What we collect

The tool only accesses the AN Central accounts that we own. It stores the access tokens those platforms issue to us, and basic account information (account ID and username) needed to publish. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of other users.

How we use it

Solely to upload and publish our own videos and captions to our own accounts.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time from the platform's app settings.
